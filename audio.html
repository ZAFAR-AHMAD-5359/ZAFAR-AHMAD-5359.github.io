01Audio restoration & investigationAnalysis & reporting

Investigating noisy, low-bandwidth recordings

Four difficult recordings needed a structured assessment of background noise, discrete events and possible speech. I combined spectral analysis with classical processing and multiple speech-enhancement models, then used independent event and voice-activity diagnostics to assess the outputs.

Problem
Interference and limited bandwidth made useful events difficult to examine.
Method
Welch/STFT analysis, filtering, neural enhancement and cross-model comparison.
Deliverable
Restoration outputs and reports distinguishing candidate speech, tonal interference and unresolved events.
PythonDeepFilterNet3MetricGAN+SepFormerSilero VAD
What the analysis can establish

Enhancement can support listening and investigation. A voice-activity score does not certify words, speaker identity or recording authenticity, and enhancement models can introduce artefacts. Reports retain these limits rather than promise recovered speech.

02Acoustic comparisonAnalysis workflows & reports

Repeatable analysis across recording conditions

Successive turntable accessory experiments needed a consistent reporting workflow. I developed tools to compare spectra, band energy, recording dynamics and channel relationships across test rounds, with one combined report covering 156 mat/clamp configurations.

Problem
Many recording conditions required consistent comparisons and readable outputs.
Method
Batch analysis, frequency-band summaries, comparative plots and structured reporting.
Deliverable
Analysis scripts, comparison figures and reports across repeated experiments.

Interpretation depends on recording controls, calibration and consistent metric definitions. Recording differences alone do not establish a hardware-performance advantage.

03Compact acoustic classificationModel & export workflow

A small sound classifier with an embedded export path

An acoustic device project required a compact model to distinguish inhalation sounds from background noise. The implementation uses FFT-band features and a 1,254-parameter convolutional network, followed by TensorFlow Lite conversion and C byte-array export.

Input
Eight frames of 32 FFT-band features.
Method
Compact CNN training and representative-data conversion.
Deliverable
Saved model, evaluation and export artefacts for device integration.

05Audio DSPMATLAB / Simulink prototype

Classical denoising with an inspectable signal path

A MATLAB/Simulink workflow compares spectral subtraction and Wiener filtering, using a noise estimate and before/after signal views to make the processing path easier to examine.

Problem
Reduce unwanted components while retaining the useful audio content.
Method
Reference-based noise estimation and classical frequency-domain filtering.
Deliverable
An inspectable model and signal comparisons for algorithm development.

The saved demonstration supports the processing workflow; it does not establish a general intelligibility benchmark.

Project approach

A clear question.
A useful result.

I scope the analysis around the decision or workflow the recording needs to support.

  1. Understand the recording and goal

    Define the input format, acquisition conditions, required outputs and criteria for a useful result.

  2. Build an inspectable baseline

    Start with signal diagnostics and a reproducible workflow; add models where they help answer the question.

  3. Deliver the workflow and its limits

    Provide the agreed outputs, supporting plots and clear notes on assumptions, failure modes and next tests.
