CARDIAC RESEARCH
The research investigates an acoustic timing reference for phase-aware murmur characterisation. In the October patient-grouped dataset evaluation, expert-reviewed phase boundaries provided useful information; the tested synthetic-ECG timing route did not establish a downstream advantage over equal-time windows. Reliable automatic timing and clinical transfer remain open research questions.
The two first-author journal manuscripts remain under revision: IEEE JBHI minor revision; EHJ Digital Health major revision (status evidence updated 9 October 2026). No accepted-publication or clinical-readiness claim is made.
