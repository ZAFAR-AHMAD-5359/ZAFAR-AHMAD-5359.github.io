CARDIAC RESEARCH
The research investigates an acoustic timing reference for phase-aware murmur characterisation. In the October 2026 patient-grouped dataset evaluation, expert-reviewed phase boundaries provided useful information; the tested synthetic-ECG timing route did not establish a downstream advantage over equal-time windows. Reliable automatic timing and clinical transfer remain open research questions.
Two first-author journal manuscripts are in revision (status as of 9 October 2026): IEEE Journal of Biomedical and Health Informatics, minor revision; European Heart Journal – Digital Health, major revision. Neither is accepted or published, and no clinical-readiness claim is made.
