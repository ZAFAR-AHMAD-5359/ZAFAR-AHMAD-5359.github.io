About

I am a cardiovascular signal-processing engineer and researcher. One question connects my work: how the heart’s electrical activity relates to its mechanical and acoustic consequences, and how that relationship can be read from the electrocardiogram (ECG), the phonocardiogram or heart sound (PCG) and the photoplethysmogram or optical pulse (PPG).

The research arc. My thesis asked whether a heart-sound recording can supply its own cardiac timing, so that murmur grade, timing and shape can be read without a simultaneous ECG. The first route, phase boundaries fixed from an ECG reconstructed by a generative adversarial network (GAN), did not separate from equal-time windows under patient-grouped evaluation, while expert-reviewed boundaries carried useful information. Separately, the reconstruction did not transfer in a blind test on new recording hardware; that result stands and is reported in the JBHI revision. The thesis and congress results are superseded: the revised manuscript, submitted to the journal and now under review, replaces them with a pre-registered, participant-grouped comparison of cardiac-phase boundary sources on the public CirCor dataset (analysis plan frozen before any arm was scored); its findings will appear here once the review concludes. The full story →

The failed transfer set my doctoral question: how do cardiac-timing models stay reliable as devices and populations change? Alongside that work I lead the technical side of a wearable ECG–PPG research prototype and am developing a probabilistic model of coupled cardiac events. Doctoral direction →

Research

MS thesis → revised manuscript

Cardiac timing from the heart sound itself

The thesis and the JBHI manuscript reconstruct an ECG-like signal from heart sounds with a conditional GAN. The EHJ-DH manuscript, revised in October 2026, asks where cardiac-phase boundaries should come from when no ECG is recorded, comparing learnt, acoustic and expert-reviewed segmentation under a pre-registered, participant-grouped design.

Where it stands · Oct 2026 In a patient-grouped evaluation, expert-reviewed phase boundaries carried useful information; the thesis’s synthetic-ECG route, with fixed phases, did not separate from equal-time windows. A pre-registered comparison of boundary sources was submitted with the EHJ-DH revision (R1), now under review (not accepted).

CAREON · NUST

Wearable ECG · PPG · motion

Within CAREON (Continuous AI-Remote E-Health Operations Network) at NUST: an ESP32 research prototype with ECG, optical-pulse and motion sensors recording on a shared clock. I set the sampling plan and specified a 16-bit external analogue-to-digital converter (ADC) fallback for acquisition precision; timing fidelity and motion sensitivity are the next tests.

The ECG-to-pulse delay includes pre-ejection time, so it is not treated as isolated pulse transit time.

Probabilistic electromechanical coupling

Copula-based coupled hidden Markov models: one chain for ECG waves (P, QRS, T), one for heart-sound phases (S1, systole, S2, diastole), and a copula for their non-linear dependence.
