What the analysis can establish

Enhancement can support listening and investigation. A voice-activity score does not certify words, speaker identity or recording authenticity, and enhancement models can introduce artefacts. Reports retain these limits rather than promise recovered speech.

Input controls, exact metrics and interpretation

Each signal is the sum of zero-phase sine waves at 100, 1000 and 3000 Hz. Their amplitudes are A: 0.20/0.10/0.00; B: 0.40/0.10/0.00; C: 0.10/0.05/0.00; D: 0.20/0.10/0.05. Duration, sample rate and frequencies are fixed, with no random noise, normalisation or processing.

RMS is the square root of mean squared sample amplitude. Level is 20 log10(RMS). The 100 Hz power share is the exact coherent Fourier-bin power divided by total mean-square power. These tones complete integer cycles, so this example needs no window correction. The downloadable report gives the calculation and assumptions.

These are digital signal metrics, rather than calibrated sound-pressure levels or loudness. The example makes no claim about intelligibility, noise reduction or turntable hardware. Its coherent-tone assumptions require separate assessment for other recordings.

Project approach

A clear question.
A useful result.

I scope the analysis around the decision or workflow the recording needs to support.

  1. Understand the recording and goal

    Define the input format, acquisition conditions, required outputs and criteria for a useful result.

  2. Build an inspectable baseline

    Start with signal diagnostics and a reproducible workflow; add models where they help answer the question.

  3. Deliver the workflow and its limits

    Agree the plots, data format and assumptions to report. Check reproducibility and output schemas against the task, then document limitations and next tests.
