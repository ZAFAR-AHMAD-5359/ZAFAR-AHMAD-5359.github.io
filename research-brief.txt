ZAFAR AHMAD - CARDIOVASCULAR RESEARCH BRIEF
Updated 9 October 2026

I am an electrical engineer and cardiovascular signal-processing researcher with an MS from NUST, conferred on 18 May 2026. My research connects ECG, phonocardiogram (PCG) and photoplethysmogram (PPG) signals with cardiac electromechanical coupling, wearable sensing and future patient-specific physiological modelling.

MS thesis: Cross-Modal Reconstruction of Cardiac Signals: Decoding ECG-PCG Electromechanical Coupling Dynamics. Supervisors: Nazia Perwaiz and Muhammad Salman Khan.

The programme investigates PCG-to-ECG timing reconstruction and phase-aware murmur characterisation. Automatically reconstructed timing remains a research question; it is not a replacement for measured diagnostic ECG. Current evaluation prioritises patient-grouped comparisons and recording-device transfer. Older results from different split protocols should not be treated as directly comparable.

Published first-author conference papers:
- ICAI 2023: Automatic Detection of Paediatric Congenital Heart Diseases from Phonocardiogram Signals. DOI: 10.1109/ICAI58407.2023.10136668.
- EUSIPCO 2024: Phonocardiogram Classification Based on 1D CNN with Pitch-Shifting and Signal Uniformity Techniques. DOI: 10.23919/EUSIPCO63174.2024.10715037.

First-author journal manuscripts:
