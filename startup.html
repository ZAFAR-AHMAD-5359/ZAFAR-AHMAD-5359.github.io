Cardiovascular research direction

Heart sounds carry
timing and structure.

The cardiac research asks whether acoustic recordings can support phase-aware murmur analysis. The diagram shows the proposed signal path, not a live diagnostic result.

The central test is whether inferred timing adds reliable information for unseen patients. Automatically reconstructed timing remains an open research question; clinical use requires further validation.

Next steps

Define the task.
Test the value.

The venture’s next steps connect project delivery with careful research development.

  1. Deliver focused engineering projects

    Build reproducible tools with agreed inputs, outputs and acceptance criteria.

  2. Resolve the cardiac timing question

    Use matched patient-grouped comparisons to test whether automatic phase estimation adds value.

  3. Test transfer between recording systems

    Evaluate the complete workflow across devices and cohorts, including failures and uncertainty.

  4. Define a research-supported product

    Work with domain collaborators to identify a useful application and its validation requirements.
