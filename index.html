02Who is Zafar?

~/zafar — whoami

I’m a cardiovascular signal-processing engineer and researcher. One question connects my work: how the heart’s electrical activity relates to its mechanical and acoustic consequences, and how that relationship can be read from ECG, heart-sound (PCG) and optical-pulse (PPG) recordings.

It began with paediatric heart-sound collection in hospitals and schools in Pakistan, where I learned what cardiologists need from a recording. Since then I have worked on deep-learning models for cross-modal ECG–PCG reconstruction and a wearable research prototype for time-aligned ECG, PPG and motion. I am also developing a probabilistic model of coupled cardiac events.

Alongside research I take on audio and biomedical signal-processing projects — spectral analysis, comparison reports, classifiers and analysis tools. For my PhD, I want to investigate whether signal-derived cardiac timing can constrain patient-specific physiological models, with uncertainty and device differences treated explicitly. My current modelling is statistical and data-driven; physiological modelling and prospective validation are skills I want to develop during a PhD. My doctoral question.

03Research focus

MS thesis · NUST

Cross-modal ECG–PCG reconstruction

A conditional GAN with multi-scale attention reconstructs an ECG-like timing reference from heart sounds; phase-aware features then target murmur presence, grade, timing and shape.

Current finding · Oct 2026 In a patient-grouped evaluation, expert-reviewed phase boundaries carried useful information; the tested synthetic-ECG timing route did not establish an advantage over equal-time windows.

Read the study →

CAREON · NUST

Wearable ECG · PPG · motion

An ESP32 research prototype with AD8232 ECG, MAX30102 PPG and MPU6050 IMU sensors. I set 250 Hz ECG and 100 Hz PPG sampling targets and specified a 16-bit external ADC fallback for acquisition precision. Timing fidelity and motion sensitivity remain to be evaluated.

The ECG-to-pulse delay includes pre-ejection time, so it is not treated as isolated pulse transit time.

Project details →

Qatar University · in development

Probabilistic electromechanical coupling

Copula-based coupled hidden Markov models: one chain for ECG waves (P, QRS, T), one for heart-sound phases (S1, systole, S2, diastole), and a copula for their non-linear dependence.
